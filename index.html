About Rimshah

Lived experience, reframed as leadership.

Rimshah Ahmed is an award-winning keynote speaker, spoken word artist, and voice coach working at the intersection of leadership, mental health, neurodiversity, faith, and human resilience. As a neurodivergent mother of three and social justice advocate, she helps people reconnect to their voice and lead with authenticity rather than performance.

Her work blends storytelling, emotional precision, and practical insight, reframing lived experience as a leadership advantage. Every engagement is immersive and emotionally intelligent, tailored to conferences, corporate events, educational spaces, and community gatherings.

"My mission is to help individuals and organizations reconnect with authentic voice through speaking, storytelling, and education, equipping them to lead with presence, honor neurodiversity and identity as strengths, and communicate in grounded, human ways."
